## 2. Is the motion encoder (S2) helping?

Feature-level probe (Step 38), no training: pooled mean/std tokens of each encoder of dino_raw (DINOv2-S S1 + our S2, both
end-to-end fine-tuned on LSVQ MOS), native + half scale, SVR. Same protocols as table 1. Tile-MAE features come from the
e2e_mos model.

| Features | LSVQ test | LSVQ-1080p | KoNViD x | LIVE-VQC x | KoNViD FT | LIVE-VQC FT | YT-UGC FT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S2 alone (our motion) | $0.745/0.747$ | $0.638/0.613$ | $0.703/0.700$ | $0.704/0.675$ | $0.699/0.680$ | $0.685/0.659$ | $0.626/0.619$ |

| **DINOv2 + S2** | $\color{blue}{\mathbf{0.891}}/\color{blue}{\mathbf{0.891}}$ | $\color{green}{\mathbf{0.834}}/\color{green}{\mathbf{0.804}}$ | $\color{blue}{\mathbf{0.879}}/\color{blue}{\mathbf{0.875}}$ | $\color{green}{\mathbf{0.844}}/\color{green}{\mathbf{0.808}}$ | $\color{blue}{\mathbf{0.883}}/\color{blue}{\mathbf{0.879}}$ | $\color{green}{\mathbf{0.863}}/\color{blue}{\mathbf{0.834}}$ | $0.848/\color{blue}{\mathbf{0.846}}$ |

- **S2 helps on every column.** It is weak alone, but adding it to DINOv2 gives +.011 to +.051 SRCC; the biggest gains are
  on LIVE-VQC (cross-dataset and FT), our weakest set against MVQA.
- **Not just more features:** adding Tile-MAE to DINOv2 instead gives only about .00–.02.
- **Not yet shown:** that the synthetic pretraining is what makes S2 useful. Controlled runs (random-init S2, fresh fusion,
  both; and a synthetic quality-ranking stage) are running (Step 41).

## 3. VideoMAE-v2-S as motion features (Step 40 probe, partial)

Can a public video model replace or add to our motion encoder? Frozen features only, no training: **V2-S** = public
VideoMAE-v2 ViT-S/16 (OpenGVLab, Kinetics-710, distilled from ViT-g; no quality labels, no synthetic data), run on our
384×216 view of each clip (never upscaled), 16 frames = every 4th frame of 64-frame spans covering the clip; per view the
pooled last-block embedding + token std and block-6 token mean/std, averaged over spans ("pad" view = 224×384, black rows
added). **dla** = our dino_long_aug model as a frozen feature extractor (S1 = DINOv2-S tokens, S2 = our motion tokens,
fusion = pooled fusion feature; native + half scale). Regressor fit on LSVQ train only, applied unchanged to each test set
(x = cross-dataset; YT-UGC x here is also cross-dataset, unlike the FT column of table 1). PLCC/SRCC.

- **dla fusion+S1S2 + V2-S beats MVQA on LSVQ test, LSVQ-1080p and KoNViD x** (features + regressor protocol; per-dataset FT

- **V2-S instead of our S2 is better on every column** (LIVE-VQC x +.022 SRCC). A public video backbone adds what our
  synthetic-pretrained motion encoder does not; Step 41 tests whether synthetic training helps in a controlled way.
