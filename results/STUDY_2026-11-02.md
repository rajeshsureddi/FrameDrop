# FrameDrop-VQA study results and frame-drop visibility, 2026-11-02

All quality cells are **PLCC/SRCC** (PLCC after a 4-parameter logistic fit). Current priority is the public sets
([`PUBLIC_2026-11-02.md`](PUBLIC_2026-11-02.md)); this page records the study numbers so far.

## Study data

FrameDrop-VQA subjective study: 1,823 4K60 videos from 35 source contents (gaming), with frame drops and compression.
Per video: MOS, and the distribution of votes over four drop-visibility grades
(not / barely / slightly / clearly noticeable).
**The study is never used to train or tune our models.** Its only use besides scoring is the per-split regressor
in Protocol A below.

## Models

The six models are the same as on the public page. They differ only in the last training stages; all MOS labels come
from LSVQ train (28,056 clips).

| Model | S1 (spatial) | Training after Stage A + 33c fusion fine-tune |
|---|---|---|
| e2e_mos | Tile-MAE | end-to-end, LSVQ MOS only, 6k steps |
| long_aug | Tile-MAE | end-to-end, LSVQ MOS only, 12k steps, random ×1.25–2 downscale augmentation (p .3) |
| stageB_seq | Tile-MAE | from e2e_mos: 2k steps of synthetic pairs only (drop / rate / severity), then 4k steps MOS only |
| stageB_seq_gv | Tile-MAE | as stageB_seq, with ~1/3 of pairs = GameVerse 4K60 gameplay drop pairs |
| stageB_alt_gv | Tile-MAE | from e2e_mos: 6k steps, every 4th step a pair batch (with GameVerse), other steps MOS |
| dino_raw | DINOv2-S (public weights) | frozen-token fusion fine-tune 20k steps, then end-to-end LSVQ MOS only 6k steps |

Stage A (all Tile-MAE models): S1 Tile-MAE and S2 motion encoder pretrained (masked reconstruction) on our own 4K60
sources; fusion trained on synthetic pair order (bitrate, distortion severity; no frame drops); then 33c = fusion
fine-tuned on LSVQ MOS with encoders frozen. Pair batches use ranking on Q + drop loss on V + 0.1 invariance.
GameVerse titles overlapping the study were excluded. Full details: [`PUBLIC_2026-11-02.md`](PUBLIC_2026-11-02.md#training).

## 1. Quality (MOS)

- **Zero-shot**: the model's own Q at native resolution against MOS, all 1,823 videos, no fitting.
- **Protocol A** (the protocol of the study baselines): the trained model is frozen and used as a feature extractor.
  Features = mean/std of S1 and S2 tokens (S1S2), optionally + the fusion feature; "half" = ×2 downscale inside the
  decoder, "ms" = native ++ half. 20 random 80/20 train/test splits grouped by source clip (no clip in both); a Ridge
  regressor is fit on each train part (alpha by grouped 5-fold CV inside it); median over the 20 test parts.
  GAMIVAL and VIDEVAL use an SVR on their own features over the same splits.

PLCC and SRCC ranked separately; $\color{gray}{gray}$ = reference row (not ranked).

## 2. Frame-drop visibility (V head)

**Labels.** Each study video has a distribution of votes over four grades (not / barely / slightly / clearly
noticeable). The label is the majority grade (a tie goes to the tied grade nearest the mean grade):
687 not, 252 barely, 144 slightly, 740 clearly noticeable.

**Protocol.** V is a continuous score, so the threshold (or cut-points) on V is fit on 4 of 5 folds grouped by source
content and applied to the 5th; repeated over 10 random fold assignments, results averaged. AUC needs no threshold.

### Reading

- Models trained on MOS only (e2e_mos, long_aug, dino_raw) have a **constant V** (their V head never saw frame drops),
  so they sit exactly at chance.
- Frame-drop training gives V a small signal: stageB_alt_gv AUC .590, balanced accuracy .570. When V is low the video is
  usually truly not noticeable (.737 recall), but 60% of noticeable videos score as low as clean ones. The barely /
  slightly grades are almost never recovered. Our older v4 (10-23) is still better (AUC .649, balanced .615).
- For reference, DOVER, FasterVQA and two VLMs (InternVL3-8B, InternVideo3-8B) are at chance on this task
  (noticed AUC ≤ .52; report §7 E12).
- On synthetic drop pairs, stageB_alt_gv's V is .92 accurate; this does not transfer to human visibility. Training
  data for *when people notice* drops is missing.
