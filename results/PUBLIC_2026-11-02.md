## Model (all our rows)

Pixels-only, three parts (see README §4):

- **S1, spatial tile encoder** (ViT-S/16): 256×256 tiles from the native-resolution frames, every 15th frame.
  - Tile-MAE (ours): masked-autoencoder pretraining on tiles of our own 4K60 source set (clean and synthetically
    distorted).
  - or DINOv2-S: the public `facebook/dinov2-small` weights (no further pretraining), patch 14 on the 252×252 centre crop of each tile.
- **S2, motion encoder** (ours, VideoMAE-style): 384×216 view of the clip, 3×4 regions; recon-only pretraining on the
  same 4K60 sources (Stage A).
- **Stage 3 fusion**: a small transformer over S1 + S2 tokens with a quality head (Q) and a drop-visibility head (V).

No study clips and no public test sets are used in any training below. The only MOS labels used are **LSVQ train
(28,056 clips)**.

## Training

Common e2e settings (`step34_e2e.py train`): AdamW, cosine, 5% warmup; per step 8 random ≤128-frame native-resolution
windows of LSVQ-train clips (32 random tiles per sampled frame); encoder lr 2e-5 with layer-wise decay 0.75, fusion
lr 3e-5, wd 0.05; MOS loss = (1 − PLCC) + 0.3·rank hinge on batch z-scored MOS; gradient checkpointing; the model is the
final step (fixed schedule, no MOS holdout).

**dino_raw** (S1 = raw DINOv2-S, S2 = our Stage A motion encoder)
1. Extract frozen S1 + S2 tokens for LSVQ train.
2. Fusion fine-tune on frozen tokens: init from our Stage A fusion (33c), LSVQ-train MOS, (1 − PLCC) + rank loss,
   20k steps.
3. End-to-end fine-tune of S1 + S2 + fusion on LSVQ-train MOS only, 6k steps (no synthetic pairs).

**dino_long_aug** (dino_raw recipe, longer and with downscale augmentation; our best public model so far)
1. Same S1 (raw DINOv2-S), S2 (Stage A motion encoder) and frozen-token fusion fine-tune as dino_raw (steps 1–2 below).
2. End-to-end fine-tune on LSVQ-train MOS only for **12k steps** (dino_raw: 6k), no synthetic pairs.
3. Downscale augmentation: each training window is, with probability 0.3, downscaled by a random factor 1.25–2 before
   tiling (S1) and before the motion view (S2), with its MOS label unchanged. This exposes the model to lower-resolution
   content like LIVE-VQC and YouTube-UGC. The same recipe on Tile-MAE (`long_aug`) gave +.01–.03.

**dino_alt_gv** (dino_raw + frame-drop training)
1. Start from dino_raw's final weights (S1, S2 and fusion, steps 1–3 above).
2. 6k more end-to-end steps with the common settings, where every 4th step is a synthetic pair batch (4 pairs) instead
   of a MOS batch: about 4,500 MOS steps and 1,500 pair steps.
   Pairs = frame-drop, frame-rate and distortion-severity orderings of our 4K60 sources; about one third are frame-drop
   pairs from GameVerse 4K60 gameplay (titles overlapping our study excluded). Pair losses: ranking on Q + drop loss on
   V + 0.1 invariance.
3. At the end, on 150 fixed validation pairs: drop accuracy .90 (Q) / .94 (V), rate ordering .96, severity .89.

**stageB_alt_gv** (S1 = our Tile-MAE, S2 = our motion encoder; adds frame-drop training)
1. Start from `e2e_mos`: Stage A encoders + 33c fusion, end-to-end fine-tuned 6k steps on LSVQ-train MOS only.
2. 6k more end-to-end steps where every 4th step is a synthetic pair batch instead of a MOS batch (4 pairs per batch).
   Pairs = frame-drop, frame-rate and distortion-severity orderings of our 4K60 sources; about one third of all
   pairs are frame-drop pairs built from GameVerse 4K60 gameplay clips (titles overlapping our study were excluded).
   Pair losses as in Stage 3: ranking on Q + drop loss on V + 0.1 invariance.

## Evaluation

All test videos are used as-is (native resolution and frame rate, no re-encode, never upscaled).

- **Direct Q**: the model's own quality output, no regressor; PLCC after a 4-parameter logistic.
- **fusion+S1S2 ms SVR**: the trained model is frozen and used as a feature extractor.
  - Features: fusion pooled feature (2×256) + mean/std of S1 and S2 tokens (1536), at native scale and at half scale
    (×2 downscale inside the decoder), concatenated ("ms").
  - **LSVQ test / 1080p / KoNViD x / LIVE-VQC x**: SVR fit on LSVQ train only, applied unchanged to LSVQ test
    (7,186), LSVQ-1080p (3,573), KoNViD-1k (1,200) and LIVE-VQC (585). The last two are cross-dataset.
  - **FT columns**: 10 random 70/10/20 train/val/test splits per dataset (KoNViD-1k, LIVE-VQC, YouTube-UGC 977);
    SVR fit on train+val (hyper-parameters by 5-fold CV inside it), median over the 10 test splits.
